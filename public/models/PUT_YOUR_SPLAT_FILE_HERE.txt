Put your exported 3D scan file in this folder.

1. Scan a room/exhibit with your phone (a slow, overlapping walkthrough video).
2. Upload that video to Luma AI (lumalabs.ai) or Polycam (poly.cam).
3. Wait for it to process — you'll get a downloadable file, usually .splat or .ply
4. Download it and drop it in this folder (public/models/).
5. Open src/main.js and change SPLAT_FILE to match your file's exact name,
   e.g. '/models/museum-entrance.splat'
6. Save, and the running dev server will reload automatically.
